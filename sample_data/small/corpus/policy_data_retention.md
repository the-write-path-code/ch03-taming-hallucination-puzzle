# Data Retention and Disposal Policy

Document ID: `DR-44`
Document type: policy

Clause DR-44 classifies customer support attachments as restricted records. Retain them for 365 days after case closure unless a legal hold applies. At the end of the retention period, delete the attachment and its derived search chunks.

A search index is not a separate retention exemption. Deleting the source without deleting its embeddings leaves an unauthorized copy of the record.
